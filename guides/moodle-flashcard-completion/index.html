How to set completion by learning progress for Moodle flashcards

LeitBox adds three completion rules to Moodle's activity completion. They look at the learning progress, not just at whether the activity was opened. You can combine them.

Updated 6 November 2026

What you need

  • Moodle 4.1 to 5.1 with LeitBox installed by an administrator (download from GitHub)
  • Editing rights in the course
  • Completion tracking switched on in the course settings

Step by step

Step 1 of 4
  1. Step 1 of 4

    Open the completion settings

    Open the activity settings and expand Completion conditions.

  2. Step 2 of 4

    Add requirements

    Choose Add requirements. Below it, the three LeitBox rules appear.

  3. Step 3 of 4

    Choose rules

    Tick one or more rules: Minimum cards answered correctly, Mastered cards (Box 5) or All cards mastered.

  4. Step 4 of 4

    Enter the number and save

    For the first two rules, enter how many cards are needed. Then click Save and return to course.

Good to know

What counts as correct

A card counts as answered correctly once it has been rated Got it at least once and is in Beginner or a higher stack.

Keep it reachable

The number of mastered cards must not be higher than the number of cards in the activity, otherwise the activity can never be completed.

Moodle 4.1 and 4.2

In older Moodle versions the completion area looks slightly different; the three LeitBox rules are the same.
